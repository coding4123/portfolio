# Delni — Mobile App

## Overview

Many small businesses in Algeria rely heavily on social media platforms
such as Instagram to promote their products. However, finding a specific
shop or product can be difficult because of inconsistent shop names,
hashtags, and limited search capabilities.

We addressed this problem by providing a centralized, category-based
directory where customers can search for products and discover businesses
that offer them.

For businesses, Delni acts as a visibility platform where they can create
their own shop profile, add products and services, and provide links to
their social media accounts.

## The Challenge

> How can we make it easier for customers to discover local businesses
> while giving small businesses the visibility they need?

Customers often spend a lot of time searching through different social
media pages to find a specific product or shop.

At the same time, many small businesses offer good products but remain
difficult to discover because they do not appear easily in traditional
social media searches.

The challenge was therefore to create a simple platform that connects
both sides: **customers looking for products and businesses looking for
customers.**

## Methodology

1. **We identified** the discovery problem faced by customers searching for local businesses on social media.
2. **We defined** the two main user groups: customers and business owners.
3. **We designed** the main user flows for searching, browsing, reviewing, and managing businesses.
4. **We organized** businesses into categories to make product discovery easier.
5. **We developed** the mobile application using Flutter and connected the required data and backend components.
6. **We implemented** product and business search with similarity-based suggestions for related or misspelled searches.
7. **We added** customer features such as favorites, ratings, and reviews.
8. **We added** business features such as shop profiles, product management, category management, and social media links.
9. **We tested** the application with early users and collected feedback to improve the experience.
10. **We prepared** the platform for growth through advertising, business subscriptions, personalized recommendations, and analytics.
