# Ratee Social Media  Design

## Overview

I created **Ratee Posts** as a collection of social media graphics designed to establish a consistent and recognizable visual language for Ratee.

I designed the posts with a focus on:

* Strong visual hierarchy
* Clear communication
* Consistent typography
* Balanced compositions
* Engaging imagery
* Consistent brand styling
* Social media-friendly layouts

Rather than treating each post as an isolated design, I focused on creating a **cohesive visual system** that allows different pieces of content to feel like part of the same brand.

## The Challenge

> How can I create social media content that remains visually engaging and recognizable while communicating different messages clearly?

Social media users consume content quickly, which means a post needs to communicate its main idea almost immediately.

The challenge was to create designs that could:

* Capture attention quickly.
* Communicate information clearly.
* Maintain visual consistency.
* Avoid unnecessary visual complexity.
* Adapt to different types of content.
* Create a recognizable visual identity for Ratee.

The goal was not simply to make visually attractive posts, but to create designs that balance **aesthetics and communication**.

## Methodology

I followed this **Ratee Posts design workflow**:

1. **I understood** the content and determined the main message that each post needed to communicate.
2. **I researched** visual references to explore relevant design styles, compositions, typography, and imagery.
3. **I defined** the visual direction by establishing the overall aesthetic and recurring design characteristics.
4. **I developed** the visual system including typography, color usage, spacing, imagery, and graphic elements.
5. **I created** initial layouts and explored different compositions for the individual posts.
6. **I established** visual hierarchy so that the primary message was immediately recognizable.
7. **I developed** multiple post variations while maintaining consistency across the collection.
8. **I refined** typography and composition through adjustments to scale, spacing, alignment, and contrast.
9. **I reviewed** the collection as a whole to ensure that the individual posts felt visually connected.
10. **I prepared** the final designs for social media presentation and publication.
