# Majlis Logo Design

## Overview

I created the **Majlis Logo** as a branding project centered around building a recognizable visual identity for Majlis.

The name *Majlis* carries a strong cultural association with gathering, conversation, and community. I designed the visual identity to communicate a sense of identity and connection while maintaining a contemporary appearance.

The design process focused on:

* Symbol development
* Cultural visual references
* Typography
* Geometric construction
* Negative space
* Visual balance
* Scalability
* Brand recognition

The result is a logo designed to function as a recognizable visual mark across different digital and branding applications.

## The Challenge

> How can I create a logo that communicates the cultural character of Majlis while maintaining a modern, simple, and recognizable visual identity?

The challenge was to create a symbol that could carry a sense of cultural identity without becoming visually complex or overly literal.

I needed the logo to balance:

* Cultural character
* Modern aesthetics
* Simplicity
* Memorability
* Typography
* Symbolism
* Scalability

The design therefore required careful exploration of both traditional visual references and contemporary logo design principles.

## Methodology

I followed this **Majlis logo design workflow**:

1. **I understood** the brand concept and identified the values and ideas the identity should communicate.
2. **I researched** cultural and visual references related to Majlis and Arabic-inspired design.
3. **I explored** visual concepts through sketches, shapes, typography, and symbolic ideas.
4. **I selected** the strongest direction based on relevance, simplicity, and distinctiveness.
5. **I developed** the symbol using geometric and vector-based construction.
6. **I explored** typography and established a visual relationship between the symbol and wordmark.
7. **I refined** proportions and spacing to improve balance and consistency.
8. **I evaluated** the logo at different scales to ensure that the core form remained recognizable.
9. **I simplified and polished** the final design by removing unnecessary elements.
10. **I prepared** the final identity for use across digital and branding applications.
