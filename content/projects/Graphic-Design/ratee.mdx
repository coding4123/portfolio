# Ratee Logo Design

## Overview

I created **Ratee Logo** as a visual identity exploration centered around creating a recognizable mark for Ratee.

The design process focused on finding a balance between:

* Simplicity
* Memorability
* Visual meaning
* Typography
* Geometry
* Scalability
* Brand recognition

I developed the final direction through multiple iterations, gradually refining the symbol and its relationship with the brand name.

My objective was to create a logo that could function independently as a visual mark while also working effectively alongside the Ratee wordmark.

## The Challenge

> How can I create a simple symbol that communicates the identity of Ratee while remaining distinctive, memorable, and flexible across different applications?

A logo needs to work at many different scales and in many different contexts.

It must remain recognizable when displayed:

* As a small social media icon
* Alongside the brand name
* On digital interfaces
* In promotional graphics
* In larger visual compositions

The challenge was therefore to avoid unnecessary complexity while creating a mark with enough character to establish a recognizable identity.

## Methodology

I followed this **Ratee logo design workflow**:

1. **I understood** the brand and defined the characteristics that the logo should communicate.
2. **I researched** visual references across branding, typography, and contemporary logo design.
3. **I generated** multiple concepts exploring symbols, letterforms, geometry, and negative space.
4. **I selected** the strongest direction based on simplicity, distinctiveness, and relevance to the brand.
5. **I constructed** the logo digitally using geometric and vector-based techniques.
6. **I explored** typography and developed a suitable relationship between the symbol and wordmark.
7. **I refined** proportions and spacing to improve visual balance.
8. **I tested** different compositions and evaluated the logo at different sizes.
9. **I simplified** the final form by removing unnecessary visual elements.
10. **I prepared** the final identity for use across digital and branding applications.
