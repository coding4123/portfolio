# Majlis — Islamic Learning Platform

## Overview

Many Muslims, particularly those living in non-Arabic-speaking countries,
can find it difficult to access reliable and structured resources to learn
about their deen.

I designed Majlis to address this gap by bringing Islamic courses and educational
content together in one accessible platform.

Users can discover courses, explore lessons, save content they are
interested in, and continue learning whenever it is convenient for them.

I designed the platform around three main principles:

- **Accessibility** — Making Islamic education easier to access.
- **Flexibility** — Allowing users to learn according to their own schedule.
- **Simplicity** — Making courses and lessons easy to discover and follow.

## The Challenge

> How can I make authentic Islamic education easier to access and learn
> from in everyday life?

Many existing learning resources can be difficult to navigate, spread
across different platforms, or difficult to fit around busy schedules.

For Muslims living in non-Arabic-speaking environments, finding reliable
and structured Islamic educational content can be an additional challenge.

I aimed to design a platform that brings learning resources together
while keeping the experience simple, organized, and flexible.
