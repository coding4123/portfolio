# BookyTrans - Website Design

## Overview

I designed BookyTrans as a digital experience that helps readers discover books across languages, compare translation options, and move from exploration to reading with less friction. The idea was to simplify a complex reading journey by making discovery, language selection, and trust signals feel natural and clear.

The product focuses on helping users find books they are genuinely interested in while giving them enough context to decide what to read next without feeling lost in too many choices.

## The Challenge

> How can a reading platform help people discover books in different languages without making the experience overwhelming or confusing?

The main challenge was creating a product that felt intuitive for readers who wanted more international variety without sacrificing clarity. I needed to balance book discovery, language choice, and trust-building so users could evaluate titles quickly and confidently.

The experience had to feel simple, inspiring, and useful from the first click, while still supporting users who wanted richer information before committing to a book.

## Methodology

1. I mapped the user journey from discovery to reading and identified the key moments where confusion or hesitation could appear.
2. I structured the information architecture around search, recommendation, language selection, and reading continuity.
3. I created wireframes to define the hierarchy of content, navigation, and trust signals for each screen.
4. I refined the interface to make the platform feel lighter, more modern, and better suited to readers browsing across languages.
5. I focused the final design on clarity, consistency, and a smooth reading-first flow that supports user confidence.
