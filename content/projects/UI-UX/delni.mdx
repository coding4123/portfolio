# Delni — Mobile App Design

## Overview

I designed Delni as a product and shop discovery mobile application developed as an academic project.

The project focused on creating a clear and intuitive experience for users who wanted to discover products and explore shops from a single mobile application. I focused on both the user experience and the implementation of the designed interface.

As a UI/UX project, my main focus was on creating a structured navigation experience, reducing unnecessary complexity, and making product discovery straightforward.

## The Challenge

> How can I make product and shop discovery simpler and more intuitive on mobile?

Finding a specific product can involve navigating through different shops, categories, and interfaces. A poorly structured experience can make this process unnecessarily difficult and force users to spend more time searching.

I needed to design an experience that organizes product and shop information clearly while keeping navigation simple and accessible.

## Methodology

1. **I identified** the main product and shop discovery problem.
2. **I defined** the information architecture and primary user flows.
3. **I designed** the mobile interface and interaction patterns in Figma.
4. **I implemented** the designed experience using Flutter and Dart.
5. **I integrated** local data management and application state management.
6. **I reviewed** the implemented flows and refined the experience.
